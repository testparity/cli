# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Parity to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind and patient with other people.
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and the sample repositories. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting form: open the **Security** tab of [testparity/cli](https://github.com/testparity/cli/security) and choose **Report a vulnerability**, starting the title with "Conduct report". Only maintainers can read these reports.

Maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly, and will keep the reporter's identity private.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that break this code. Depending on severity, they may give a private warning, ask for a public apology, or temporarily or permanently ban someone from the project.
